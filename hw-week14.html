2

Grammar review. Choose the correct option.

Выбери правильный вариант. Будь внимателен: это повторение всех грамматических тем.

  1. I (play / am playing) football every Saturday.
  2. Look! She (climbs / is climbing) the tree!
  3. You (must / mustn't) cross on the red light.
  4. My brother is good at (draw / drawing).
  5. The kitchen is (big / bigger) than the bathroom.
  6. They (always go / are always going) to the park on Sunday.

6

Speaking task. Name 5 + 5 + 5.

Запиши аудио на 40–50 секунд. Назови 5 действий в парке, 5 мест в городе, 5 комнат дома.

Plan for your speaking:
  • Start: "Let me name some words I know."
  • 5 park actions: "In the park I can..., ..., ..., ..., ..."
  • 5 places in town: "In my town there is a..., a..., a..., a..., a..."
  • 5 rooms: "In my house there is a..., a..., a..., a..., a..."
  • End: "That's a lot of words!"
Example: "Let me name some words I know. In the park I can hop, skip, walk, climb, and laugh. In my town there is a bank, a hospital, a library, a café, and a supermarket. In my house there is a kitchen, a bedroom, a bathroom, a living room, and a hall. That's a lot of words!"
