6

Speaking task. Tell about your favourite day.

Запиши аудио на 30–40 секунд. Расскажи о своём любимом дне недели и любимом предмете.

Plan for your speaking:
  • What is your favourite day of the week?
  • What subjects do you have on that day?
  • What is your favourite subject? Why?
  • What do you do after school on that day?
Example: "My favourite day is ___. On ___ I have ___ and ___. My favourite subject is ___ because ___. After school I ___."
